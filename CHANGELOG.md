# Gamepad Camera Offset

## 1.2.0

- Hold & drag works on a controller: bind it to the right stick's click, then move the side offset with the left stick or the D-pad
- While it's held, the left stick moves the camera, not your character; in a fight, the D-pad's left and right step it instead
- A Drag speed slider: how fast hold & drag moves the side offset, with the mouse or the left stick
- Stop at center (off until you turn it on): hold & drag stops at the center for a moment, with a short buzz on the controller, so you know it's centered
- A Step size slider (20% until you change it): how far a Left or Right key, or the D-pad while hold & drag is held, moves the side offset

## 1.1.0

- Keys for the side offset, in Key Bindings > AddOns:
  - Hold one (a mouse button too, like the middle one) and move the mouse left or right
  - Or step it left and right; held, it keeps going (works on a controller)
- None is bound until you bind it
- A Key Bindings button on the settings page opens them

Thanks to stozeel on CurseForge for suggesting it: an excellent idea.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Side offset: the camera over your right or left shoulder
- Tilt: your character sits lower on the screen, so you see more ahead
- Tilt when looking down: how much of the tilt stays when you look down
- Works on a controller and with keyboard and mouse
- Every setting starts at the game's own value: nothing changes until you move one
- Settings in Options > AddOns > Gamepad Camera Offset, or /gco
