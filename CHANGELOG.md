# Gamepad Camera Offset

## 1.1.0

- Keys for the side offset, in Key Bindings > AddOns:
  - Hold one (a mouse button too, like the middle one) and move the mouse left or right
  - Or step it left and right; held, it keeps going (works on a controller)
- None is bound until you bind it
- A Key Bindings button on the settings page opens them

## 1.0.1

- New icon: a tauren with horns, hair and a cape, seen from behind

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Side offset: the camera over your right or left shoulder
- Tilt: your character sits lower on the screen, so you see more ahead
- Tilt when looking down: how much of the tilt stays when you look down
- Works on a controller and with keyboard and mouse
- Every setting starts at the game's own value: nothing changes until you move one
- Settings in Options > AddOns > Gamepad Camera Offset, or /gco
